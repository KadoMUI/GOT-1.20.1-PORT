# 0.5I — Resources and Roads

This checkpoint extends the Flora and Atmosphere source without changing the
terrain height sampler, hydrology rules, Planetos preset, or Winterfell entry.

## Underground resources

- Restores the original dirt, gravel, and seven country-rock vein passes.
- Generates copper, tin, coal, iron, sulfur, saltpeter, salt, lapis, silver,
  gold, and eight legacy gem veins.
- Restores mountain glowstone/cobalt deposits, Tarth sapphire beds, ocean salt,
  Sothoryos/Ulthos obsidian gravel, and Valyria's rare Valyrian ore.
- Keeps the original ordinary gem factor and the richer Sothoryos, Ulthos, and
  mountain multipliers.
- Adds the fourteen missing legacy ore/gem block identities, original textures,
  loot behavior, mining tags, Forge ore tags, and salt/cobalt furnace recipes.

## Roads

- Projects all 170 original ROAD splines and 112 waypoint LINKER splines onto
  the completed surface at the original seven-block width.
- Selects dirty, paving, sandy, cobbled, Asshai, snowy, or Sothoryosi materials
  from the local authored biome.
- Clears generated foliage above routes and adds Reach Fire Field roadside
  flowers without changing terrain elevation.
- Builds oak water crossings with edge rails and periodic supports.
- Retains the five WALL splines in the shared map catalogue. Physical Wall and
  Five Forts construction remains with their regional structure milestones.

World generation changes require fresh chunks.
