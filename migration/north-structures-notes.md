# North Structures checkpoint

This milestone ports the complete legacy North structure-spawner subset and
the original North biome placement rules onto the 0.5I Resources and Roads
baseline.

## Included

- 18 legacy `SpawnedID` variants (15–28 and 309–312)
- North village, small-town, town, fort and hillman layouts
- Eleven market-stall roles and three village-farm roles
- Twenty-nine fixed waypoint settlements from the legacy fixer
- Random 12-chunk settlement grid, North watchforts and North Barrows mounds
- Rotation, terrain foundations, chunk clipping and structure loot
- Deterministic NPC/banner markers for the future population milestone

No NPC entity is created by this checkpoint. Marker roles retain the intended
locations and identities without introducing unfinished faction dependencies.
