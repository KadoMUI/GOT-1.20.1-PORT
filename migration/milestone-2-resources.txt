Milestone 2: Resource foundation

Added modern registrations and assets for raw tin, silver, dragonglass (legacy obsidian shard), and Valyrian steel.
Added Bronze, Silver, Alloy Steel, and Valyrian Steel storage blocks.
Added silver ore generation and raw-ore drops.
Added Forge material tags and reversible nugget/ingot/block recipes.

Intentional deferrals:
- Alloy Steel has no ordinary crafting recipe. Its original alloy-forge production system must be ported first.
- Valyrian Steel has no ordinary crafting recipe. Its lore-specific/original acquisition system must be ported first.
- Dragonglass shard acquisition is deferred until the original obsidian/dragonglass mechanics are reviewed.
- raw_tin, raw_silver, and deepslate_silver_ore currently use temporary textures derived from existing assets.

0.5I update:
- Planetos underground generation now restores the legacy common, gem, mountain, ocean, Sothoryos, Ulthos, Tarth, and Valyria deposits.
- The animated Valyrian ore block now generates rarely below Valyria's volcano, but the ore-to-Valyrian-Steel acquisition chain remains intentionally deferred to its original lore system.
