# Project Thrones 0.9.9 - Material Armor Recipes Pass 4

Adds material armor recipes without changing the existing faction-table armor system.

## Plate armor
Standard vanilla armor silhouettes using full ingots for Alloy Steel, Bronze, Copper, and Valyrian Steel.
Gold continues to use vanilla gold armor rather than adding a duplicate GOT plate set.

## Chainmail armor
Uses the custom ingot+nugget patterns supplied for helmet, chestplate, leggings, and boots for:
- Alloy Steel
- Bronze
- Copper
- Gold
- Valyrian Steel

Copper uses minecraft:copper_ingot + got:copper_nugget. Gold uses vanilla gold ingots/nuggets. Valyrian armor item IDs are got:valyrian_* while their crafting material is got:valyrian_steel_ingot / got:valyrian_steel_nugget.

## Intentionally untouched
- Anonymous Mask remains uncraftable.
- Asshai Mask crafting was not added; intended acquisition is Necromancer drop.
- Robert Baratheon's helmet left for a later pass.
- Sandor Clegane's helmet crafting was not added; intended acquisition is Sandor Clegane drop.
- Existing GOT faction armor crafting behavior is unchanged.
